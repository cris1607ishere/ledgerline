⚡ Concurrency & Race Condition Suite (Simulations)

Fire genuine simultaneous HTTP requests at the backend in parallel to verify database row-locking, idempotency deduplication, and deadlock immunity.

1. Overdraft Attack Race

Fires 10 simultaneous 100 INR transfers out of Bob's account (500 INR balance). Proves pessimistic locks reject losing requests and balance never drops below 0.

2. Idempotency Key Race

Blasts 20 simultaneous duplicate requests carrying the exact same Idempotency-Key. Proves only 1 transaction settles and money moves once.

3. Bidirectional Deadlock Race

Fires 10 A→B and 10 B→A transfers at the exact same millisecond. Proves deterministic UUID lock ordering completely avoids deadlocks.
